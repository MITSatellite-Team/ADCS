Use the helpers while soldering

Search a reference or LCSC code, select the row, then use Zoom to part. Bottom views already show the physical underside. Green 1 marks pad 1; check the actual chip/LED polarity marking. Purple labels identify 3.3 V wire targets. Check off fitted parts; export the checklist when handing work over.

  • Follow the helper’s selected part, including replacements. Main C78/C79/C80 are 10 µF, 25 V; fit the specified F1 fuse. No extra capacitor or gate-driver supply rewire is required.
  • IMU U1 sits at the existing 173° angle. Align to its lands, not the PCB edge.
  • Inspect stencil openings before paste: keep R37/R38 clear and check the ESP32 center pads have divided openings. Reflow SMT one side at a time with support for the populated side. Use the paste/component reflow instructions; inspect hidden-pad parts carefully. Solder through-hole parts and wires afterward.

Three required modifications

  1. Main: leave R37/R38 absent. Add insulated 30 AWG wires from C44 pad 2 → R37 pad 1 and C44 pad 2 → R38 pad 1. Their pad-2 ends remain 5 V; do not bridge them. Keep R47, C45 and C46 fitted. Select C44/R37/R38 in the helper to locate the pads.
  2. Encoder: before fitting U3, remove the small top trace section marked red in its helper. Confirm H1.5 still connects to U3.1, U3.2 and C3 supply, but is isolated from CN6.6. Add main CN9.1 (3.3 V) → encoder H1.5; main C44.2 is an alternative source. Leave H1 empty. Keep all seven encoder-cable wires intact.
  3. Programmer cable: disconnect pin 1 of one six-wire cable, insulate both ends separately and label it PROGRAMMER ONLY. Verify pin 1 is open and pins 2–6 remain one-to-one. The IMU’s six-wire cable stays intact. No programmer PCB cut is needed.

Before handoff: inspect orientation, bridges and hidden-pad joints; verify the two CAN jumper connections, encoder isolation and cable continuity. Keep the motor disconnected. Power/firmware commissioning is a separate step: the stock firmware is not the one-motor test program.
